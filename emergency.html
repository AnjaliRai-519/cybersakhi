📞 Emergency Help

Quick guidance during a cyber incident

🚨 Financial Cyber Fraud

If money has been transferred because of an online fraud, contact your bank or payment provider immediately through its official channel.

In India, you can also use the official cybercrime reporting system to report the incident.

🇮🇳 Cyber Crime Reporting

Use the official Government of India cybercrime reporting portal for reporting cybercrime.

🛡️ If Your Account Is Hacked

  • Change your password immediately.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Sign out unknown devices.
  • Check recent account activity.
  • Contact the platform's official support.

💳 If Money Was Lost

  • Contact your bank/payment provider immediately.
  • Keep transaction details safely.
  • Take screenshots of relevant messages.
  • Do not communicate further with the scammer.
  • Report the incident through official channels.
