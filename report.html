🚨 Report Cyber Crime

Know what to do when something goes wrong

⚠️ If You Are a Victim

If you have experienced an online financial fraud, act quickly and contact your bank or payment provider through its official support channel.

📸 Preserve Evidence

Keep useful evidence such as:

  • 📱 Screenshots
  • 💬 Chat or SMS messages
  • 📧 Suspicious emails
  • 🔗 Suspicious URLs
  • 💳 Transaction details
  • 📞 Phone numbers used by the scammer

🛡️ Before Reporting

  • Do not delete important evidence.
  • Do not send additional money.
  • Do not share OTP or passwords.
  • Secure your affected accounts.
  • Contact your bank quickly if money was involved.

🇮🇳 Official Help

For cybercrime reporting in India, use the official Government of India cybercrime reporting service.
