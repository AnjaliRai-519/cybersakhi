📚 Common Scam Types

Learn about common online scams and simple ways to stay safe.

💳

UPI & Payment Scam

Scammers may pretend to be buyers, sellers, customer-support agents or bank representatives and try to make you send money.

🛡️ Never share your UPI PIN or OTP.
💼

Fake Job Scam

Fraudsters may offer attractive jobs and ask for registration, training or processing fees.

🛡️ Be cautious if a job requires upfront payment.
🎁

Lottery & Prize Scam

A message may claim that you have won a prize and ask for money or personal information to receive it.

🛡️ Don't pay money to claim an unexpected prize.
🔐

Phishing Scam

Fake emails, messages or websites may try to steal passwords, banking details or other sensitive information.

🛡️ Check the website and sender before entering information.
📱

Fake KYC Scam

Fraudsters may claim that your bank, wallet or SIM will be blocked unless you complete an urgent KYC process.

🛡️ Use only official apps and websites for KYC.
💕

Romance Scam

Someone may build an online relationship and later ask for money, gifts or financial help.

🛡️ Never send money to an online contact you haven't verified.
📈

Investment Scam

Fake investment opportunities may promise unusually high or guaranteed returns.

🛡️ Verify investment platforms before investing money.
🎣

Social Media Scam

Fake accounts, giveaways and messages can be used to collect personal information or money.

🛡️ Verify accounts and avoid suspicious links.
