🛡️ Cyber Safety Tips

Stay Smart • Stay Safe

🔐 1. Protect Your Password

Use strong and unique passwords. Avoid using your name, birthday or simple numbers.

🔢 2. Never Share OTP

Banks and legitimate services should not ask you to share your OTP with strangers.

🔗 3. Avoid Suspicious Links

Don't click unknown links received through SMS, email or social media.

📱 4. Secure Your Social Media

Use privacy settings and enable two-factor authentication whenever available.

💳 5. Be Careful With Online Payments

Verify the recipient before making a payment. Never share your PIN or banking password.

⚠️ 6. Beware of Fake Job Offers

Be cautious if someone demands money, OTPs or personal documents for a job or internship.
